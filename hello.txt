Scorpio / N remained Mahindra’s highest-selling nameplate with 15,576 units sold in August. This was a 58.29% YoY increase over 9,840 units in August 2025. Compared to July’s 16,009 units, sales declined 2.70%. Scorpio range accounted for 26.29% of Mahindra’s August sales, meaning roughly one in every four Mahindra vehicles sold during the month carried the Scorpio nameplate.
